How will you begin to dream something no machine can dream?

Something a machine doesn't have is memories of a personal story. The experiences and perspectives one gains throughout childhood, teenagerhood, and adulthood--a machine is developed with lines of code and pre-said ideas. My dream is through my own personal recommendations developed from years of seeing the world from my own viewpoint over the past 18 years, something a machine won't be able to replicate.

If memory is built from shared stories, which dreams do you want writtein in the past? What remains of you when all your stories are rewritten?

I want the dreams that I'm drawn to for a good night's rest. The late night laughter and early morning breakfasts with smiling faces. The time that was spent with warmth in the heart. The past should be looked back upon with grace, instead of negativity. Beyond the stories that shape me as a person, what remains is the foundation to many of my stories: my physical body. It affects many parts of how I interact with the world--my physical limits, how others perceive me, how I perceive myself, and the standards enforced upon me. It's then how I respond in which my stories start to form.

If you seek to realize your hopes through AI, how will you ensure that it remains faithful to your will?

AI can be programmed to remain faithful to one's will, but the slightest wording in its program that can be interpreted through an open-ended means can change everything. Just as AI is able to stick to the rules, AI is also able to find loopholes. Hope shouldn't become reliant on AI. It should be powered through human willpower, and willpower alone.

Which relationships will make your life meaningful and magical? How do you nurture them?

The relationships that serve as a pillar of meaningfulness in my life are the ones that turn on a light when it's dark, provide warmth when it's cold, and act as a roof when it's raining. They cause smile wrinkles and make tomorrow worth looking forward to. When it's time to call it a day, you agree with them for "5 more minutes". And in return, I do the same unconditionally.

What are the aesthetics of the places that you live your life and how did they come to be that way?

I find places that give a calm and peaceful aura. Where I can plant roots, build relationships, but also have the freedom to explore. I grew up in an environment that was loud, and constantly energetic, and I felt burnt out at a young age. So now, I search for calmness, like a slow-moving creek.
How do you spend your time? What stories do you seek out, whether to tell or listen to?
I spend my time taking in my surroundings. How did they come about? Who might've put effort into it? Who else appreciates it? There are stories behind everything. Nothing is happenstance. And it's important to know this. The effort put into daily things that go appreciated or unappreciated, the services we use. Everything had someone taking key details to perfect it for us.
How will it sound? What will the rhythm of your day be?
At the beginning of my day, my eyes will first dart to the window to see if it's daybreak yet. If I see rays of sunlight pushing through the curtain, I'll immediately wonder which of my t-shirts I should wear. If there's no hint of light, I feel a strong desire to lay back in bed, but I arise nevertheless. My day can be represented through a calm hum, and intensifying when I experience energetic emotions.
How will you move through the world?
Similar to the rhythm of my day, I'll move through the world along to a calm hum. When it's time to put in effort, my hum will intensify, supporting my energy. I'll blend in with the movement of the crowd, but will be a friendly face within my circle. The world has plenty of special people, but few people have special people.
